ABOUT FONTAINE

Fontaine, known as the Nation of Justice, is an enchanting hub of art, high fashion, and clockwork engineering surrounded by pristine waters. Renowned for its grand European-inspired boulevards, elevated water-canal trams, and thriving theatrical culture, it offers travelers a sophisticated and picturesque escape. From sparkling fountains to bustling waterfront cafés, every corner of Fontaine celebrates elegance, innovation, and spectacle.

YOUR STOPS

A closer look at the destinations waiting for you on this expedition.

COURT OF FONTAINE

Surrounded by crystal-clear waterways and elevated Aquabus lines, the Court of Fontaine dazzles visitors with its grand stone arcades, boutique shops, and intricate clockwork marvels. Stroll down the sunlit Vasari Passage, enjoy freshly baked pastries at a sidewalk café, or watch friendly clockwork automatons patrol the clean avenues. As evening falls, warm streetlights illuminate the brass fixtures and shimmering canals across the city. Every moment in the capital feels like stepping into a living work of art.

OPERA EPICLESE

Situated across the water on Erinnyes Island, the Opera Epiclese is a breathtaking monument to Fontainian architecture, performing arts, and civic drama. Marvel at its soaring vaulted ceilings, ornate stained-glass windows, and stately marble plaza overlooking the surrounding coastline. Guided tours allow travelers to explore the grand foyer and learn about the iconic performances and historic trials held within its walls. The sheer scale and elegance of the venue leave every visitor in awe.

LUCINE FOUNTAIN

Framed by manicured gardens and flowing waterways just outside the opera house, the Lucine Fountain is one of Fontaine's most beloved scenic landmarks. Walk along the circular stone promenade, toss a coin into the sparkling waters, and capture stunning photographs with the grand spires of the island rising behind you. Gentle water sprays cool the afternoon air while visitors gather to relax and enjoy the peaceful ambiance. It is a must-visit spot for travelers seeking quiet beauty and timeless charm.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What should I wear for the Fontaine tour? +

Smart-casual attire and polished, comfortable walking shoes—Fontaine’s grand boulevards and Opera Epiclese favor neat fashion, while a light trench coat protects against coastal sea breezes.

What should I bring with me? +

Bring your FONT-05 confirmation, Aquabus (water-canal tram) transit pass provided at check-in, an umbrella, a camera, and Mora (local currency) for boutique cafés and pastry shops.

Is the tour suitable for families and children? +

Yes! Smooth paved avenues, scenic Aquabus (water-canal tram) rides, and open plazas around the Lucine Fountain make Fontaine effortless and enjoyable for families with strollers or older relatives.

How much walking is involved? +

Light to moderate walking (2 to 3 hours daily) along flat stone promenades, supplemented by frequent Aquabus (water-canal tram) transfers.

Can I explore on my own during the trip? +

Yes. Day 2 afternoon along the waterfront and Day 4 afternoon in the Quartier Lyonnais (artisan quarter) are completely free for independent café hopping and boutique shopping.

Are there any rules or protocols I should follow during the tour? +

Maintain strict silence inside the Opera Epiclese if a court trial or performance is underway, keep hands clear of active clockwork gardemek (automated patrol robots), and validate your Aquabus pass before boarding.
